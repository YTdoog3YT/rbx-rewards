Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At RBX Rewards, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by RBX Rewards and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We do not require passwords for account creation. The personal information we collect includes:

  • Username: Required to identify your account and track your earned points.
  • Payment Information (PayPal Email): Collected strictly when you request a withdrawal. For fraud prevention and to protect your funds from unauthorized access, this email address is securely and permanently linked to your account upon your first withdrawal.
  • IP Address & Device Data: Collected automatically to prevent fraud, detect VPN/Proxy usage, and ensure the quality of traffic for our advertising partners.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website.
  • Process your reward payouts accurately and securely.
  • Find and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and malicious activity.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website to improve user experience.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads to our users based on their previous visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy. We also work with third-party Offerwall providers (such as CPX Research) who may use their own cookies to track survey completion statuses. We have no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
