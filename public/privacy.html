Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At RBX Rewards ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy and digital safety of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by RBX Rewards and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We operate on a strict data-minimization principle. We never ask for or collect gaming account passwords. The limited information we collect includes:

  • Public Username: Provided voluntarily by you to identify your community profile, display your progress on leaderboards, and track your accumulated points.
  • Reward Delivery Details (Electronic Wallet Email): Collected strictly when you submit a reward claim request. For security and fraud-prevention purposes, the payout email address provided during your first claim is permanently associated with your profile.
  • Local Storage & Technical Data: We use browser localStorage to remember your language preferences, cookie consent status, and active username session. Our servers also log standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps) to prevent automated abuse, detect VPN/Proxy connections, and maintain platform security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our community hub, global chat, and leaderboards.
  • To verify task completions and process user reward claims accurately.
  • To respond to user inquiries submitted through our Support ticket system.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions, multi-accounting, and unauthorized access.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on select pages of our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising and the use of the DART cookie by visiting the official Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.
  • Our website also integrates third-party interactive quest and market research providers. When you voluntarily open a partner task wall, those third-party partners may use their own cookies and tracking identifiers in accordance with their respective privacy policies to verify task completion. RBX Rewards has no access to or control over third-party cookies.

4. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about your profile, request corrections, or request the deletion of your profile data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our administration team via the Support tab on our website or through our official Discord server.

5. Children's Information

Protecting children while using the internet is a priority. Our platform is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data on our website, please contact us immediately via Support and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
