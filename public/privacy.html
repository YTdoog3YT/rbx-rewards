Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At RBX Rewards ("we", "us", or "our"), available on rbx-rewards.pl and in our Android app, one of our main priorities is the privacy and digital safety of our users. This Privacy Policy describes what information RBX Rewards collects, how we use it and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We follow a strict data-minimization principle. We never ask for or collect gaming account passwords. The limited information we collect includes:

  • Nickname: Provided voluntarily by you to identify your profile, display your progress on leaderboards and track your points. Your nickname is public: it is shown on leaderboards, in the global chat and in the list of latest payouts (together with the amount and date).
  • PIN: Your 6-digit PIN is never stored in readable form. We keep only a one-way cryptographic hash of it, which cannot be turned back into the PIN.
  • Reward Delivery Details: Your Revolut username (Revtag), collected only when you submit a payout request. For security and fraud prevention, the Revtag from your first payout is permanently associated with your profile. When you order a game bundle, we also store the identifiers needed for delivery, such as your game nickname or ID and your Discord username.
  • Activity Records: A history of points earned, deducted and withdrawn, so that you and our team can see what happened on your account.
  • Local Storage & Technical Data: We use browser localStorage to remember your language, your cookie consent and your login session (a session token that expires after 6 hours). Our servers also log standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and timestamps) to prevent abuse, detect VPN/Proxy connections and keep the platform secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, operate and maintain the platform, global chat and leaderboards.
  • To verify task completions and process payout requests.
  • To deliver game bundles and confirm deliveries.
  • To respond to questions sent through our support system, Discord server or email.
  • To detect, investigate and prevent fraud, multi-accounting and unauthorized access.

3. Google AdSense, Partners & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on selected pages of our website, such as blog articles. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising and learn how Google uses data by visiting the official Google advertising privacy page.
  • Our platform also integrates third-party task and market research providers (such as TimeWall and CPX Research). When you open a partner wall, we pass your nickname to that partner so that completed tasks can be credited to you, and the partner may use its own cookies and identifiers in accordance with its own privacy policy. RBX Rewards has no access to or control over third-party cookies.

4. Mobile App & Google AdMob

Our Android app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including rewarded ads that give you points.

  • AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalization in your device settings.
  • When you finish a rewarded ad, Google sends our server a confirmation containing your nickname, so that the reward can be credited to the correct account. The app also stores your nickname on your device to keep you signed in.

5. Data Retention

Login sessions are deleted automatically after 6 hours. Account data, activity history and payout records are kept while your account exists, because they are needed to prevent fraud and to answer questions about past payouts. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

6. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about your profile, to request corrections or to request deletion of your data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

7. Children's Information

Protecting children online is a priority. Our platform is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data on our platform, contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

8. Contact

Our main contact channel is our official Discord server. You can also write to us at rbxrewards.kontakt@gmail.com.

9. Consent

By using our website and app, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
