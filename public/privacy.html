Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

At RBX Rewards, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by RBX Rewards and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via our Support Ticket system or Discord Server.

1. Information We Collect

We collect strictly necessary information to provide you with our services:

  • Roblox Username: Collected solely to track your points and process your Robux payouts. We NEVER ask for your password.
  • PayPal Email Address: Collected only if you choose to withdraw your earnings via PayPal.
  • Log Files & IP Addresses: Automatically collected for security, fraud prevention, and to ensure you receive correct offers based on your region.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website.
  • Process your Robux and PayPal withdrawals.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse of our reward system.

3. GDPR Data Protection Rights (EU Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. RBX Rewards is fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure ("Right to be forgotten"): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

4. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among other rights, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data. RBX Rewards does not sell your personal data to third parties.

If you make a request regarding your GDPR or CCPA rights, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us via our Discord Server support channel.

5. Third-Party Partners and Offerwalls

We use third-party advertising and survey networks (such as TheoremReach, CPX Research, Jitscape, and Google AdSense) to provide you with earning opportunities. These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on RBX Rewards, which are sent directly to users' browser.

They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that RBX Rewards has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers. We encourage you to review their respective Privacy Policies.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. RBX Rewards does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
