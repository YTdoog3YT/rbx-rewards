Discord for Gamers: How to Use Our Community Server Safely

Discord is the main place where the RBX Rewards community meets. This is where we post announcements, confirm deliveries and answer questions. Here is how to get the most out of it without putting your account at risk.

What Discord is

Discord is a free chat app for communities. A server is a community space, divided into text and voice channels on different topics. You can use Discord in a web browser, on a computer or on a phone, and one account works everywhere.

What you will find on our server

  • Announcements about new features, maintenance breaks and changes to the rules.
  • Promo codes that give extra points. They are usually limited, so it is worth turning on notifications for this channel.
  • Delivery confirmations for payouts and bundles, so everyone can see that rewards are really sent.
  • Support from our team if something goes wrong with points, a payout or a bundle.

You can join using the official invite link. Use only this link or the one shown on rbx-rewards.pl, because scammers create copies of popular servers with similar names.

Secure your Discord account first

  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). In User Settings, open the account section and enable an authenticator app. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot log in without the code from your phone.
  • Use a unique password that you do not use in any game or other website.
  • Limit private messages. In the privacy settings you can block DMs from people who only share a server with you. This cuts off most spam.
  • Never scan a QR code that someone sends you "to verify" your account. Logging in with a QR code gives the other person full access.

Common scams to recognise

"Free Nitro" links. A message from a friend with a link to free Discord Nitro is very often sent from a hacked account. The page looks like Discord but steals your login.

"I reported you by accident". A stranger claims your account will be banned and sends you to a fake "Discord staff" member who asks for your password or a payment. Discord staff do not contact users like this.

Fake admins. Someone copies the avatar and name of our administrator and offers "faster payouts" or "double points". Our team will never ask for your password, your PIN or a payment, and will never ask you to log in through a link sent in a DM.

Downloads. Files called "game cheats", "Robux generators" or "screenshots.exe" are usually malware. Do not open them.

How deliveries work on Discord

Some bundles, for example Star Stable gift codes, are delivered as a private message from our administrator after the bundle is fully paid. Before you use a code, check that the message comes from the administrator listed on our server, not from a look-alike account. If anything looks suspicious, ask in the support channel or email rbxrewards.kontakt@gmail.com.

Good manners help everyone

Keep conversations friendly, do not spam referral links in other channels and describe support problems with details: your nickname, what you did and when. A clear report is solved much faster than "it doesn't work". For more tips about staying safe, read our online safety guide.
