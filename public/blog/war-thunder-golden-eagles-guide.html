War Thunder for Beginners: How to Spend Golden Eagles Wisely

War Thunder is free to play, but its economy confuses many new players. Golden Eagles are the premium currency, and spending them in the wrong place is one of the most common beginner mistakes. Here is how the system works and how to get real value.

The three currencies

  • Research Points (RP) are earned in battles and used to unlock new vehicles and modifications in the tech tree.
  • Silver Lions (SL) are the main in-game money. You use them to buy researched vehicles, train crews and pay for repairs after battles.
  • Golden Eagles (GE) are the premium currency. You get them mainly by purchase, gift codes or occasional events, and they unlock things that save time.

Understanding this split is important: RP and SL come from playing, while Golden Eagles are limited, so every Eagle should go where it gives the most benefit.

What Golden Eagles can buy

  • Premium account for a chosen number of days. It increases the RP and SL you earn from every battle.
  • Premium vehicles that do not need to be researched and usually earn more than regular vehicles.
  • Talismans, which give a regular vehicle the same earning bonus as a premium one.
  • Research acceleration and conversion of experience, which finish research instantly.
  • Extra crew slots, crew training and decorations.
  • The paid part of seasonal Battle Passes, when one is running.

What is worth it for a beginner

Wait before spending anything. Play the first ranks for free. They are short, and you will quickly learn whether you prefer ground vehicles, aircraft or ships and which nation you like. Eagles spent on day one often go to a vehicle you stop using a week later.

Premium account is the best value if you play regularly. It boosts every vehicle and every battle. Activate it when you know you will have time to play, because the days count down whether you play or not.

One premium vehicle at a rank you enjoy can be a good second purchase. Choose a vehicle in the nation and type you actually play, and preferably one that matches the rank you are researching, so it helps you progress.

Avoid research acceleration early on. Low-rank vehicles are researched quickly anyway, so paying to skip them gives very little value.

Watch for sales

Gaijin regularly runs sales around holidays and anniversaries, with discounts on premium vehicles, premium accounts and Eagles. If nothing urgent is needed, waiting for a sale stretches the same amount of Eagles further. Always buy from the official in-game store or official Gaijin websites.

Protect your Gaijin account

Turn on two-step verification for your Gaijin account, for example with the official Gaijin Pass app, and never share your password or verification codes. Offers of "free Golden Eagles" through generators or suspicious websites are scams designed to steal accounts.
