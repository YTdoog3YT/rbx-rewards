Roblox Lag and Low FPS: A Practical Fix Guide

Stuttering, rubber-banding and delayed jumps can ruin any Roblox game. The good news is that most problems have simple causes. The first step is to find out whether your device or your internet connection is to blame.

Two different problems: FPS and ping

FPS (frames per second) tells you how many images your device draws every second. Low FPS means the game looks choppy even when nothing is happening online. It is a problem with your computer or phone.

Ping is the time, in milliseconds, that data needs to travel to the game server and back. High ping means that your actions appear late, other players teleport and you get pulled back after running. It is a problem with the connection.

The fixes are completely different, so check which problem you have before changing anything.

How to check

  • Press Esc to open the Roblox menu, go to Settings and turn on Performance Stats. Small panels with live numbers will appear on your screen.
  • On a computer you can also press F9 to open the Developer Console, which shows errors and memory usage. This is useful if a single game is lagging while others run fine.

If FPS drops while ping stays stable, focus on graphics. If ping jumps up and down, focus on the network.

Fixing low FPS

  • Lower graphics quality. In Settings, switch Graphics Mode to Manual and move the Graphics Quality slider down a few steps. Big, detailed maps benefit the most.
  • Close background programs. Browsers with many tabs, video streams and recording software use the same processor and memory as the game.
  • Update graphics drivers on a PC. Use the official website of your graphics card manufacturer or the system update tool.
  • Plug in your laptop. On battery, many laptops reduce performance to save power.
  • Let the device cool down. Phones and tablets slow down when they get hot. Remove a thick case and avoid charging while playing.

Fixing lag and high ping

  • Use a cable if you can. A wired connection is more stable than Wi-Fi. If you must use Wi-Fi, sit closer to the router.
  • Stop large downloads and updates on other devices in your home while you play.
  • Restart your router if the connection has been unstable for a while.
  • Try another server. Leave and join the same experience again. You may land on a less crowded or closer server.
  • Turn off VPNs while gaming. They add extra distance to every packet.

When the problem is the game itself

Sometimes one experience lags for everyone because of heavy scripts or a very large map. If other games run smoothly on the same device, the cause is probably the game. In that case the only real fixes are lower graphics settings or waiting for the developer to optimise it.

Stay away from "FPS boosters"

Downloads that promise to "unlock FPS" or "remove lag" are a popular way to spread malware and steal accounts. Using unofficial programs that modify the Roblox client can also break the Roblox Terms of Use. The safe settings above solve the vast majority of problems without any risk.
