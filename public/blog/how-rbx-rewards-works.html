How RBX Rewards Works: Points, Payouts and Bundles Explained

New players ask us the same questions every week: where do the points come from, are they real Robux, how fast are payouts and why did a reward disappear? This guide answers all of them in one place.

Where the points come from

RBX Rewards does not create value out of nothing. When you complete a survey, test an app or finish a task on a partner offerwall such as TimeWall or CPX Research, the advertiser pays the offerwall, the offerwall pays us, and we pass a share of that money to you in the form of R$ points. This is the same model used by every legitimate "get-paid-to" website.

Partners confirm each completed task automatically through a secure server notification. That is why points usually appear in your balance within seconds, without you having to send screenshots or proof.

R$ points are not Robux

It is important to say this clearly: R$ points are our own loyalty points, not Roblox currency. We are an independent community project and we are not affiliated with Roblox Corporation. Points live only on our platform and can be exchanged for the rewards listed on the website.

How payouts work

  • Exchange rate: 1 R$ point equals $0.025 USD.
  • Minimum payout: 1 R$, so you never wait weeks to cash out.
  • Payout method: a transfer to your Revolut account using your Revtag (your @username in the Revolut app).
  • Security lock: the Revtag you enter for your first payout is permanently linked to your account. If someone ever got into your account, they still could not send your money to themselves. A change is possible only through support.
  • Manual review: every payout is checked by a real person before it is sent, which protects honest players from fraud.

Game bundles

Instead of a cash payout you can spend points on bundles: Robux packs, War Thunder Golden Eagles or Star Stable Star Coins gift cards. Each bundle has a progress bar. You deposit points into it whenever you like, and once it is fully paid our team delivers it and confirms the delivery on our Discord. Bundle prices include a small activation fee that covers card fees and currency conversion.

A few rules keep this fair: you can have up to two active bundles per category, you can withdraw deposited points as long as the bundle is not fully paid, and if a bundle is ever rejected, every deposited point is returned to your balance.

Why points can be reversed

Survey partners check the quality of answers, sometimes days or weeks later. If they detect random answers, speeding through questions, a VPN or multiple accounts, they cancel the reward and we have to deduct those points. Referral bonuses linked to that task are reversed too. Every deduction is visible in your account history, so you always know what happened. Our survey guide explains how to avoid reversals.

Extra ways to earn

  • Referrals: you receive a 15% bonus from the quest points earned by friends you invite.
  • Levels and the daily wheel: regular activity unlocks small bonuses.
  • Promo codes: we publish them on our Discord server.

Keeping your account safe

Every account is protected with a 6-digit PIN. We never ask for your Roblox password and you should never give it to anyone. Your login stays active for up to six hours, so always click Logout on a shared computer. More tips are in our online safety guide.
