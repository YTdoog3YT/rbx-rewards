Online Safety for Gamers: Protect Your Accounts and Your Money

Game accounts are valuable. They hold years of progress, items and sometimes real money, which makes them an attractive target. Most account thefts are not clever hacking but simple tricks. Learn to spot them and your accounts become much harder to steal.

1. Passwords: unique and long

The most common way accounts are stolen is password reuse. A small website leaks its database, criminals take the email and password pairs and try them on Roblox, Discord, email and games. If you used the same password everywhere, all of those accounts fall at once.

  • Use a different password for every important account, especially your email.
  • Make it long. A phrase of several random words is easier to remember and harder to guess than a short password with symbols.
  • A password manager, including the one built into your browser or phone, can create and remember passwords for you.

2. Turn on two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a second code, usually from an app on your phone, when you log in from a new device. Even if someone has your password, they cannot get in without that code. Turn it on for your email first, because your email can reset every other password, and then for Roblox, Discord, Gaijin and other game accounts. Never tell anyone a 2FA code, not even someone claiming to be support.

3. Recognise phishing

Phishing is a fake message or website that imitates a real service to steal your login. Warning signs:

  • The address is slightly wrong: extra letters, a different ending or a misspelled brand name.
  • The message creates urgency: "your account will be deleted in 24 hours", "claim your prize now".
  • You are asked to log in through a link from a chat, comment or email instead of opening the site yourself.

The safest habit is simple: do not log in through links. Type the address yourself or use a bookmark.

4. "Free Robux" and similar scams

Robux generators, free item websites, "double your Robux" offers and fake giveaways do not work. They exist to collect passwords, push malware or make you complete endless tasks for nothing. Roblox currency comes only from official sources. The same applies to "free Golden Eagles", "free Nitro" and similar offers.

On reward platforms such as RBX Rewards you earn points for real tasks paid by advertisers. A trustworthy platform never asks for your game password. We only ask for your Roblox nickname and protect your account with your own PIN.

5. Downloads and browser extensions

Cheats, "FPS unlockers", mod menus and unknown browser extensions are a common way to install malware that steals saved passwords and login cookies. Download software only from official sources and keep your system and browser up to date. Read our Roblox performance guide for safe ways to improve FPS.

6. Protect personal information

Do not share your home address, school, phone number, ID documents or bank details in games or chats. Be careful with people you only know online, even if they are friendly, and especially if they ask to move the conversation to another app or keep it secret. If you are a younger player and something makes you uncomfortable, talk to a parent or another adult you trust, and use the block and report tools.

7. If an account gets stolen

  1. Secure your email first: change its password and turn on 2FA.
  2. Use the official account recovery page of the affected service and contact its support.
  3. Change passwords on every account that used the same password.
  4. Scan your computer with trusted antivirus software if you downloaded something suspicious.
